# Art Style Specification & Prompts for 28 Memory Illustrations by Chapter Cluster (Level 4)

This document records the unified art style specifications for the 28 pure-visual memory illustrations mapped to Chapter Clusters across all 5 Books (180 Chapters).

---

## Two Unified Hand-Drawn Art Styles

### Style A — Book I (Illustrations 01–08 — Chapters 1–67): 8/8 Completed
- **Medium Simulation:** Black charcoal combined with warm golden-ochre chalk pastel on lightly textured ivory fine-art paper, with edges softly smudged into the white paper background.
- **Artistic Spirit:** Rustic, hand-sketched strokes with natural pastel and charcoal softness rich in primordial mythic light; strictly no text, numbers, hard borders, or digital crispness.

### Style B — Books II to V (Illustrations 09–28): 20/20 Completed (Illustrations 09–28)
- **Medium Simulation:** Monochrome sepia-ink and warm umber watercolor wash combined with soft pencil sketching inside a natural fading circular/oval vignette on ivory fine-art paper, blending gently into pure white borders.
- **Artistic Spirit:** Quiet, contemplative, generous white paper breathing space, humble hand-drawn warmth like an artist painting directly with watercolor brush and graphite pencil; strictly no written letters, numbers, or hard frames.
