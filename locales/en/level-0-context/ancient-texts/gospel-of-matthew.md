# Gospel According to Matthew

---

## Chapter I: The Royal Lineage and the Birth at Bethlehem

**1.** The book records the lineage passed down from ancestor Abraham, who fathered Isaac, Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers. It passed down through Boaz and Ruth, and through Jesse, who fathered **King David**. From King David through his son **King Solomon**, the royal line continued through the kings of Judah down to the exile in Babylon, and onward to Jacob, the father of **Joseph**.

**2.** Now the birth of **Jesus** in the land of Judea took place in this way: gentle **Mary** was betrothed to **Joseph**, a descendant of the royal house of David. In a dream, a heavenly messenger appeared and told Joseph not to hesitate to welcome Mary into his home, for the child about to be born carried a high calling to save humankind from its wrongs, fulfilling the ancient prophecy of **Emmanuel**—meaning *"The Most High is with us."*

**3.** Waking from his dream, Joseph did just as the messenger instructed, tenderly welcomed Mary into his home, and watched devotedly over her until the day she gave birth to a son in the town of **Bethlehem** in Judea, and they named the child **Jesus**.

---

## Chapter II: The Star in the East, the Three Wise Men, and the Flight by Night

**1.** Shortly after Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea during the reign of King **Herod**, behold, **wise men from the East** arrived in Jerusalem after a long journey, asking: *"Where is the newborn King? For we saw **his star rising in the East**, and we have come to pay him homage."*

**2.** Hearing these words, King Herod was startled and filled with fear for his throne, and all Jerusalem was stirred with him. Gathering the scholars learned in the ancient writings, the king demanded where the promised one was to be born, and was told: in Bethlehem of Judea, just as the prophet of old had written. Herod secretly summoned the wise men to learn the exact hour the star had first appeared, then deceitfully bade them search carefully for the child and bring back word so he might go and pay homage as well.

**3.** Leaving the palace and setting out toward Bethlehem, the wise men rejoiced with exceeding joy when **the very star they had seen in the East went before them**, until it stopped and hovered directly above the place where the child was. Entering the house and seeing the child with his mother Mary, they bowed down to the ground in humble reverence, opened their treasure chests, and presented three precious gifts: **pure gold, frankincense, and myrrh**. Warned afterward in a dream not to return to Herod, the wise men took another route home to their eastern lands.

**4.** Scarcely had they departed when a heavenly messenger appeared in a dream and woke Joseph in the middle of the night: *"Rise up, take the child and his mother, and **flee to Egypt** at once, and stay there until I send you word, for Herod is about to search for the child to harm him."* Rising immediately in the dead of night, Joseph led his wife and child onto the road into Egypt, remaining there for several years until King Herod died.

**5.** After Herod's death, a messenger appeared again in a dream in Egypt telling Joseph to bring his wife and child back to their native land. Hearing that Herod's son now ruled over Judea, Joseph felt wary and turned northward into Galilee, settling in a small hillside village named **Nazareth**.

---

## Chapter III: The Voice in the Wilderness and Baptism in the River Jordan

**1.** Many years later, **John**, clad in a cloak woven of camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist and living frugally on locusts and wild honey, stepped forth into the wilderness of Judea crying aloud with strength: *"Turn your hearts anew, for the reign of heaven has drawn near!"* He was the very voice crying out in the wilderness to prepare a straight highway, just as the prophet Isaiah had foretold long ago.

**2.** Crowds poured out from Jerusalem, all Judea, and the whole region along the Jordan to listen, confess their wrongs, and receive the washing of water in the **River Jordan**. John spoke humbly to all, saying that he baptized only with outward water to remind people to change from the depths of their hearts, whereas One far mightier was coming after him, whose sandal straps he was not even worthy to untie.

**3.** Then **Jesus** departed from the village of Nazareth in Galilee and walked down to the banks of the River Jordan to meet John and ask for baptism. Hesitant, John answered: *"It is I who need to be baptized by you; why do you come to me?"* Yet Jesus replied gently: *"Let it be so now, for it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness."*

**4.** Hearing this, John consented. The moment Jesus stepped up out of the waters of the Jordan, behold, **the heavens were opened**; Jesus beheld the Spirit descending from above **like a dove and alighting upon him**, while a voice resounded from high heaven declaring deep love and complete delight in the beloved Son.
