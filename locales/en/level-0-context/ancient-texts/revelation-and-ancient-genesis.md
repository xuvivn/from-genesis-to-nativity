# Book of Revelation and Ancient Creation Writings

---

## Chapter I: The Realm of Primal Light and the Pride of the Morning Star

**1.** Long ages before there were sun, moon, stars, or solid earth beneath our feet, only the high heavenly realms existed, bathed in boundless light and the flawless harmony of countless angels. Rank upon rank of angels served before the throne of light in pure gladness, knowing neither shadow nor tears.

**2.** Among the brightest of the angels shone a luminous spirit like the **Morning Star** at daybreak, arrayed in radiance more dazzling than all his companions. Yet one day, gazing upon his own brilliance, a proud thought sprouted quietly within his heart: *"I will ascend above the highest clouds, set my throne higher than the stars, and sit equal beside the Most High!"*

**3.** The very moment that self-centered pride awoke, the pure light within the Morning Star grew dim and murky, darkening into the first shadow severed from the primal source of light. He sought to conceal that dark stain and drew part of the angelic hosts after his rebellious ambition, letting heavy silence intrude for the first time into the songs of heaven.

---

## Chapter II: The Battle in Heaven and the Dragon Cast into the Abyss

**1.** When every call to return to the light was firmly refused, a **great war broke out in heaven**: the Archangel **Michael** and his faithful angels rose up to battle against the **Great Dragon**—that ancient serpent full of pride—and all its host of followers.

**2.** Swords of radiant light flashed across the churning skies as Michael led the heavenly legions to halt the plot to seize the throne of light. The Dragon and its legions roared fiercely in counterattack, yet could not withstand the pure strength of truth, and no place was left for them any longer in the realm of light.

**3.** The Great Dragon and all its fallen host were defeated and hurled straight down into the dark abyss below. Again the Dragon surged upward, striving to scale the threshold of light to spoil the creation about to be formed, yet Michael's sword of light struck down once more and drove it back into darkness—drawing a clear boundary between the Kingdom of Light above and the Abyss below, and opening the way for the warm days when the earthly world would be created.
